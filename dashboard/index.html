Before you recordUse a pseudonymous participant ID - never a name, email, or phone number. Upload only after a signed consent record exists outside this dashboard. Recordings are queued for human review; they are not automatically used for training.
Consent acknowledgement is required before recording.

Required review path

  1. Obtain informed consent outside the app.
  2. Record and submit the sample with an exact transcript.
  3. Review transcript quality and remove any identifiers.
  4. Validate and convert audio to 16 kHz mono.
  5. Add only approved rows to the original-corpus manifest.
  6. Freeze speaker-disjoint splits before training.

This local intake accepts WebM, OGG, M4A, or WAV recordings up to 25 MB. Use a private authenticated deployment with encrypted storage before collecting recordings from the public internet.
